Clinical notes & referencesTap to expand

This is a transparent clinical-reference calculator, not a substitute for individualized insulin settings, clinical judgment, device-specific bolus-calculator logic, or a patient's prescribed hypoglycemia, exercise, steroid, or sick-day plan.

The activity and corticosteroid percentages are editable heuristics. Exercise-related bolus reductions vary substantially with timing and intensity. Glucocorticoid-related insulin needs vary with the steroid agent, dose, timing, and duration.

The basal titration helper uses a published adult type 2 diabetes approach based on the median of 3 consecutive fasting glucose values and a default 2-unit adjustment.
