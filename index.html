Clinical notes & referencesTap to expand

This tool is intended as a transparent clinical-reference calculator for outpatient rapid-acting insulin dosing. It does not replace individualized insulin settings, clinical judgment, device-specific bolus-calculator logic, or a patient's prescribed hypoglycemia/sick-day plan.

UCSF Diabetes Teaching Center describes carbohydrate coverage as carbohydrate grams divided by the insulin-to-carbohydrate ratio, and correction dosing from the difference between current and target glucose divided by the insulin sensitivity factor. UCSF also describes the Rule of 500 and Rule of 1800 as starting estimates that require individualization.
